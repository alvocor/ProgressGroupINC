# Progress Group Inc. — Brand Guide

- **Status:** Initial original identity for website implementation; owner may revise before public release
- **Prepared:** 2026-07-19
- **Brand idea:** Clear direction, connected work, and practical forward motion

## Identity Concept

The mark combines an open pathway, connected checkpoints, and an upward movement inside a stable rounded frame. It is intended to communicate progress without relying on a generic arrow-only consulting logo. The restrained geometry supports professional use at favicon, navigation, document, and presentation sizes.

The horizontal logo uses the mark with the name **Progress Group Inc.** The website may render the name as HTML next to the standalone mark when that improves responsiveness and typography.

## Color System

| Token | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Progress Navy | `#0B1F33` | Primary brand field, headings, dark sections |
| Harbor Blue | `#1E4E79` | Supporting depth, links, data lines |
| Forward Teal | `#1B7F7A` | Progress paths, interactive accents, positive emphasis |
| Signal Gold | `#D8A541` | Selective highlight, active state, focal point |
| Warm Canvas | `#F5F2EA` | Main light background |
| Mist | `#DCE7E7` | Dividers, cards, quiet backgrounds |
| Slate | `#60758A` | Secondary text and metadata |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | High-contrast content on dark fields |

Use Signal Gold sparingly. Large text fields and body copy should use the navy/white or navy/canvas combinations. Verify actual contrast in the implemented design rather than assuming token names guarantee accessibility.

## Typography Direction

- **Display/headings:** a confident geometric or humanist sans serif such as Manrope, with system fallbacks.
- **Body/UI:** Inter or a high-quality system sans-serif stack.
- **Tone:** concise, assured, practical, and readable; avoid exaggerated corporate language and dense all-caps text.

Font files or external font services are not authorized by this guide. The implementation session must choose a privacy-appropriate loading strategy and verify licensing and performance.

## Visual Language

- Layered pathways, grids, connected nodes, and open space.
- Precise geometry softened by rounded corners and subtle curves.
- Deep navy fields balanced with warm neutral surfaces.
- Teal indicates connection and forward motion; gold identifies one important transition or focal point.
- Motion should feel deliberate: slow path drawing, gentle reveals, and small depth changes rather than constant movement.
- Respect reduced-motion preferences and keep all information available without animation.

## Logo Usage

- Preserve clear space equal to at least one quarter of the mark width.
- Do not stretch, rotate, add shadows, recolor individual paths arbitrarily, or place the logo over visually noisy imagery.
- Use `logo-horizontal.svg` on wide navigation or presentation surfaces.
- Use `logo-mark.svg` for compact navigation, social avatars, or branded decorative moments.
- Use `favicon.svg` only at small browser-icon sizes.
- For very small rendering, prefer the mark rather than squeezing the full wordmark.
